# Sharp Favard decay with uniform marginals

Sergiu Alexandru Bîsceanu

For the $n$ parallelograms obtained by matching the equal subintervals of the two horizontal edges of the unit square, we prove that the minimum area of their union is of order $\frac{1}{\log n}$, as conjectured by Kaczynski.

The paper also proves the same order for Kaczynski’s original problem, in which the top intervals can slide independently. For the permutation model, an exact crossing identity and a multiscale construction characterize how rapidly the projections can shrink in directions approaching the coordinate axes.
