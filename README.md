# Sharp Favard decay with uniform marginals

Sergiu Alexandru Bîsceanu

For the $n$ parallelograms obtained by matching the equal subintervals of the two horizontal edges of the unit square, we prove that the minimum area of their union is of order $\frac{1}{\log n}$, as conjectured by Kaczynski.

The paper also proves the same order for Kaczynski’s original problem, in which the top intervals can slide independently. The five-page proof gives matching bounds at each scale for weighted projections near the coordinate axes. At the critical angular weight, it determines the additional factor of $\log\log n$.
